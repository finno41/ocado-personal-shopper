# Recipe library — browse for inspiration

A human-friendly index of every recipe that has come in via a URL. Skim it when you
want ideas. Each entry: name, link, a one-line description, and a rating out of 5.
The full ingredient list and base servings for each recipe live in the matching file
under `recipes/`, not here — this is just an index for browsing and rating.

`Rating: —` means unrated. At the start of the next shop you'll be asked to rate any
unrated recipes.

Newest entries go at the top.

> This is the shipped example. Copy it to `recipe-urls.md` and start adding your own.
