# Consumables — replenishables suggestion list

This is the running list of household consumables to *offer* at each shop. It grows
over time: whenever a new consumable is mentioned during a shop, append it here so it
is suggested next time. Keep it de-duplicated and roughly grouped.

Format: one item per line under a heading. A `default: yes` tag means "tick this by
default when offering the list" (things you nearly always need). Everything else is
offered unticked.

> This is the shipped example. Copy it to `consumables.md` and make it your own.

## Bathroom
- Toilet roll
- Hand soap
- Toothpaste

## Kitchen & cleaning
- Washing-up liquid
- Dishwasher tablets
- Kitchen roll
- Bin bags
- Cling film / foil

## Laundry
- Laundry detergent

## Staples
- Milk  (default: yes)
- Butter
- Eggs
- Bread
