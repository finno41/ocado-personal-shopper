session every time. Grows over time: whenever you give feedback after a shop, append it
here (de-duplicated, grouped) so it carries into future briefs.

## Store
- Ocado.

## Choosing products
- Prioritise good price and good reviews when choosing between comparable products.
- Pick the closest reasonable pack size to the amount needed. If nothing is near, go
  **bigger, never smaller**, and note the surplus in the clarifications at the end.
- For consumables, buy in bulk if it works out cheaper per unit.

## Substitutions
- If an item is unavailable, suggest a better alternative (price / reviews / closer
  size) flagged for approval rather than silently swapping.
